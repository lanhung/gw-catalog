## 2. Reviewer's main points (full report is in the chat history; essentials)

1. Not novel vs Offermans et al. (arXiv:2411.12453): same kind of time-domain
   network, already compared with a CQT classifier (Goyal et al. LensID). Lines
   339–341 of the submission apparently say the network is related — **unverified**.
2. Unrealistic simulations: uniform masses 10–100 Msun, uniform-comoving z < 1,
   lens at z_s/2, uniform y in [0.01, 0.3], point masses as a lens class.
   Reviewer suggests `ler` (https://ler.hemantaph.com/) and LVK populations.
3. Cost argument: Barsode et al. (arXiv:2412.01278, 2605.10333) reach ~54%
   efficiency at FPP 1e-6 in < 1 s per pair; PI-ResNet's speed does not compensate.
   Compare at O4 sensitivity (apples to apples), consider FPP, efficiency,
   per-event preprocessing and downstream cost together; place the method in
   workflows (LensingFlow arXiv:2507.20256; LVK O4 lensing arXiv:2512.16347).
4. Conciseness; define metrics in Table 3; remove old Sec. 5.2 once populations are realistic.
5. Minor: cite Barsode; line 83 (Ezquiaga et al. 2023 is not a Bayesian classifier);
   rename "selection function" → "lensing selection function"; common x-axis in Fig. 6.
6. Data editor: LICENSE + Zenodo DOI (real names, ORCIDs), CSV behind Fig. 6.

## 4. Decisions / recommendations made

- **Repositioning**: not "a better classifier" but a calibrated strain-level
  **pre-filter** between catalogue-level time/sky pre-selection and the
  posterior-overlap / Bayes-factor stage; report efficiency–FAP curve over
  1e-1…1e-4; emphasise no PE posteriors needed and 0.5 ms per-event preprocessing.
- **Point mass**: demote, don't delete (Sec. 2b of the response draft): keep only as
  a lens-profile robustness test on the realistic source population with p(y) ∝ y,
  evaluated by the galaxy-trained network; remove from abstract / headline table /
  conclusions. Deleting outright is also defensible (reviewer asked for it).
- "PI" described as physics-inspired design choices (code imposes no lensing equations).
- Writing: physics first, statistics as GW-search analogies (background from
  unrelated pairs = time slides; frozen thresholds = blind analysis), ML metrics
  (AUC, McNemar) to an appendix, glossary appendix.
